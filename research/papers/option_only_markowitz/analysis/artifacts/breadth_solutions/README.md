# Breadth Solutions Diagnostic

This bundle records the follow-on breadth/capacity experiments for the option-only
Markowitz paper. It is diagnostic evidence, not a replacement for the headline paper run.
It supersedes the earlier baseline-only probes under `../breadth_experiment/`.

## Bottom Line

At `$1M` NAV, the large-universe Greek-Markowitz book can be made net-positive when the
cost ledger uses exact panel CBBO where available and a point-in-time inferred CBBO proxy
where historical bid/ask rows are missing. With VIX included, the 57-group book moves from
net Sharpe `-1.837` in the uncapped paper configuration to `+1.499` in the
diagonal-residual, N-scaled, structural-only regularized/capped configuration and beats
the best capped-naive book by `1.232` net Sharpe. Without VIX, the 56-name book reaches
`+0.551` net, essentially tying capped equal-risk naive at `+0.550`; the no-VIX optimizer
edge is therefore not established.

The stale Cboe delayed-chain assumption file is no longer consumed by the regenerated
P1/P2/P3 tables. The code still rejects off-hours snapshots and requires full requested
symbol coverage before overwriting that optional audit file, but the checked-in breadth
net results use the offline inferred-CBBO proxy instead of weekend/holiday current-chain
quotes or 10%/15% class defaults.

The no-VIX 8-name equity baseline is already exact for spread inputs: the `orig`
source-coverage row is entirely `panel_cbbo`, covering 5,777 cost rows, 49 asset IDs, and
all eight baseline underlyings. `orig+VIX` uses the same exact equity-option CBBO rows,
while VIX option spread rows use the inferred liquid-option CBBO proxy because the local
VIX chain cache does not contain bid/ask CBBO fields.

Above roughly `$2M`, representative month-end option liquidity is the binding constraint.
At `$5M+`, hard full deployment is capacity-infeasible across the tested configurations
and participation rates; relaxed positive rows deploy only a negligible fraction of NAV.

## Robustness Validation

The production-candidate validation run is under `robustness/` and is generated by:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m research.papers.option_only_markowitz.analysis.breadth_robustness_experiment --configs all --out-dir research/papers/option_only_markowitz/analysis/artifacts/breadth_solutions/robustness
```

It locks the E1 capped candidate (`historical_weight=0`, `shrinkage_to_zero=0.75`,
diagonal residual covariance, N-scaled shrinkage, `X=0.05`, `$1M` NAV) rather than
reselecting knobs inside folds. The checked full run uses 12 chronological groups,
66 CPCV splits, 78 total CV/PBO splits per config, purge/embargo of one month, 1,000
resampled historical paths, 200 refit paths, 1,000 repriced synthetic paths, circular-block
and GARCH-style path simulations, drawdown breach simulations, reality-check inference,
and rolling 36-month monthly OOS refits.

`robustness/breadth_validation_summary.md` is the compact status page. It reports:

| Config | Deployable | Verdict | Net Sharpe | Net Sortino | MC refit net p05 | Rolling net Sharpe |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| orig | false | diagnostic_capacity_infeasible | 0.720 | 1.629 | 0.540 | 0.584 |
| orig+VIX | true | pass | 1.287 | 3.256 | 1.118 | 1.007 |
| larger | true | mixed | 0.551 | 1.196 | 0.491 | 0.466 |
| larger+VIX | true | pass | 1.499 | 4.004 | 1.262 | 1.217 |

The robustness spread-source audit passes: no row uses `current_cboe_liquid_quote`, and
no row uses `default` spread assumptions. Repriced synthetic net paths subtract resampled
realized full-cost drag; they are a documented historical-cost overlay, not synthetic
NBBO/CBBO. CPCV complete-path net Sharpes are negative for all E1 configs despite positive
gross medians, so the paper treats CPCV as a cost-timing fragility diagnostic rather than
a tradable OOS claim. Some fold-local and rolling refits are capacity-infeasible even when
the static full-sample row is deployable; those rows remain diagnostic.

## Decision Table At `$1M`

| Config | GM paper net | Best capped-naive net | Regularized+capped net | Gross/edge note |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| orig+VIX | -1.196* | -0.853* | 1.287* | net-max gross 1.613 |
| larger+VIX | -1.837* | 0.266* | 1.499* | optimizer edge +1.232 |
| orig | -1.443 | 0.350 | 0.720 | cap budget below 1 NAV |
| larger | -1.991* | 0.550* | 0.551* | optimizer edge +0.001 |

`*` marks configs with `inferred_cbbo_proxy` rows. `p3_spread_source_coverage.csv`
reports 23,740 such equity-option cost rows across 213 asset IDs and 47 added underlyings,
with median relative spread `1.97%`; VIX proxy rows have median relative spread `2.53%`.
The `orig` row has no fallback spread source; it is `panel_cbbo` for the eight equity
underlyings. Gross and capacity metrics are cleaner large-universe signals because they do
not depend on the spread proxy.

## Files

- `p1_regularization_results.csv/json` and `p1_summary.md`: estimator regularization
  sweep across default, covariance-shrinkage, residual-covariance, structural-only mean,
  and combined arms.
- `p2_liquidity_results.csv/json`, `p2_caps_detail.csv`, and `p2_summary.md`: pre-trade
  liquidity-cap sweep over participation rates and AUMs.
- `p3_combined_results.csv/json`, `p3_spread_source_coverage.csv`, and
  `p3_decision_table.md`: combined regularization plus liquidity-cap decision table and
  spread-source audit.
- `current_option_spread_assumptions.csv` and `current_option_spread_fetch_audit.csv`:
  optional Cboe delayed-chain liquid-quote audit files. They are not consumed by the
  regenerated P1/P2/P3 breadth tables; future builds must be run during regular option
  market hours and off-hours snapshots are rejected.

## Method Notes

- P1: structural-only forecasts (`historical_weight=0`, `shrinkage_to_zero=0.75`) are the
  main fix. They help at 56/57 names and hurt at eight names, consistent with a `T/n`
  estimation-noise mechanism.
- P2: caps use `min(0.18, X * train_volume * mark * 100 / NAV)` and are computed from
  train-window volume only.
- P3: caps bind net position `abs(q_i)`, not split long/short solver legs. This avoids
  offsetting pseudo-cash consuming scarce contract capacity.

## Caveats

- Large-universe net rows remain proxy-based where historical CBBO is missing and
  `inferred_cbbo_proxy` appears in `p3_spread_source_coverage.csv`; production deployment
  still needs matched historical or live executable NBBO/CBBO and broker-fill evidence.
- Best-knob selection is in-sample across the diagnostic grid.
- The P1/P2/P3 decision table is still one static train/test split with static test-period
  weights; the `robustness/` runner locks E1 and evaluates it across fold-local CV,
  resampled, synthetic, and rolling diagnostics.

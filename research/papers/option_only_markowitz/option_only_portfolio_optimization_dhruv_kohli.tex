\noindent{\bfseries\color{journalgreen} Abstract}

We repair an option-only mean--variance implementation and reassess what its historical
evidence can support. Option positions are premium-funded, expiring cashflows. For the
fixed Greek loading matrix, the covariance retains factor/residual cross terms rather
than assuming an OLS projection that the code does not estimate. The repaired R1
allocation maximizes expected return net of asymmetric costs and a variance penalty,
permits cash, and imposes CVaR, stress, margin, collateral, assignment, integer, and
point-in-time liquidity limits before a position is accepted. The covariance factors are
limited to delta, centered quadratic spot returns, and vega; skew and tail enter premia
and stress tests. We make no claim that these standard mathematical ingredients are new.
The original E1 Sharpes are retained as retrospective development evidence, not headline
proof: E1 was selected after inspection of the post-2020 window, and its VIX-enabled CPCV
wealth paths are absorbed at zero in March 2020 despite positive return-based Sharpe.
Under R1's hard survival gate that is a failure. All existing observations are labeled
development data, and confirmatory claims require 36 future untouched monthly returns.
The separately versioned R1.1 development arm raises the risk ceiling to 25 percent,
pursues but does not force 50 percent gross, and specifies a close-to-next-open VIX-40
exit rule. Its event-date return remains unscored until matched licensed CBBO is available;
its EGARCH overlay fails a prespecified promotion gate.

\vspace{0.26cm}
\noindent\colorbox{impactshade}{%
  \begin{minipage}{0.94\textwidth}
  \textbf{\color{journalgreen} Key findings and claim boundary}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item R1 includes the covariance cross terms omitted by E1 and prices predictable
    costs inside a uniquely scaled utility objective.
    \item Gross premium is an upper bound; cash replaces split-leg ``burn'' and infeasible
    integer books fail closed to cash.
    \item Any zero-wealth, margin, collateral, or integer-feasibility failure overrides
    Sharpe. The legacy VIX CPCV paths therefore fail the repaired survival standard.
    \item Existing results are retrospective development evidence. A prospective freeze
    manifest requires 36 untouched monthly observations for confirmation.
    \item R1.1 does not attain its 50 percent deployment target under the retained
    constraints; its VIX risk-off arm is unscored without executable event quotes.
    \item The licensed-quote audit leaves headline R1.1 orig+VIX Sortino at 2.275 under
    modeled costs and 2.052 under observed touch fills, with 94.1 percent entry coverage.
    \item R1.1 is the paper's final development model; R2 remains a gate-failed diagnostic.
  \end{itemize}
  \end{minipage}}

\section*{Data availability statement}
The reproduction package contains code, generated tables, figures, verification outputs,
schemas, and artifact hashes. Raw licensed OPRA/Databento data are not redistributed. The
repository instead ships the audit's aggregate artifacts under
\path{analysis/artifacts/execution_audit/} and the acquisition summary with request hashes.
Entry-window costs and decision marks for held positions are audited against licensed
consolidated quotes. The baseline eight equity-option spread rows use local historical
panel CBBO. Broad-name and
VIX spread rows that lack matched historical CBBO use the documented inferred CBBO proxy
and are labeled as execution-sensitivity evidence. Exact VRO/SOQ settlement files are
versioned by source hash before VIX option rows enter the reported tables.

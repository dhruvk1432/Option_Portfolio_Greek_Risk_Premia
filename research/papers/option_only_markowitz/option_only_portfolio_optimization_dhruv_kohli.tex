\noindent{\bfseries\color{journalgreen} Abstract}

This paper presents one retrospective development result. The High Ceiling Allocator on
the eight-name plus VIX universe returns 16.0 percent annualized with a Sortino ratio of
2.275 and a maximum drawdown of 17.1 percent under frozen modeled costs. Its Sortino ratio
is 2.052 at observed touch fills, with 94.1 percent entry coverage in a licensed-quote
audit. The allocator combines premium accounting, a complete Greek factor and residual
covariance, asymmetric costs, cash, and hard survival gates. The result is not a
confirmatory claim. Confirmation requires 36 untouched monthly observations beginning
2026-07-31 under frozen code, data rules, and hashes.

\vspace{0.26cm}
\noindent\colorbox{impactshade}{%
  \begin{minipage}{0.94\textwidth}
  \textbf{\color{journalgreen} Key findings and claim boundary}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item The eight-name plus VIX High Ceiling Allocator is the headline development
    result because it survives the frozen risk and observed-quote checks.
    \item The Survival Allocator retains Greek factor and residual cross terms, prices
    predictable costs inside utility, permits cash, and rejects infeasible integer books.
    \item The Sharpe Prototype fails the survival standard when its VIX-enabled CPCV paths
    absorb at zero in March 2020.
    \item Every reported path remains retrospective. The prospective freeze sets the
    boundary for any later confirmatory claim.
  \end{itemize}
  \end{minipage}}

\section*{Data availability statement}
The reproduction package contains code, generated tables, figures, verification outputs,
schemas, and artifact hashes. The repository does not redistribute raw licensed
OPRA/Databento data. It instead ships aggregate artifacts in the execution-audit
directory and an acquisition summary with request hashes.
The audit checks entry-window costs and decision marks for held positions against licensed
consolidated quotes. The baseline eight equity-option spread rows use local historical
panel CBBO. Broad-name and VIX spread rows without matched historical CBBO use the
documented inferred CBBO proxy. The paper labels them as execution-sensitivity evidence.
The pipeline versions exact VRO/SOQ settlement files by source hash before VIX option rows
enter the reported tables.

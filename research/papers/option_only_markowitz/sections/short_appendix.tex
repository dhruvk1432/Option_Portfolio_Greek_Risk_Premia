\section{Technical details and relocated evidence}

This appendix records the mathematical steps needed to audit the implementation and the
wide exhibits that the main reading path omits. The arguments are standard. We include
them to make the code-to-equation mapping explicit.

\subsection{Funded option scale and covariance}

Let \(w\) denote signed premium weights and let residual cash be the numeraire. If the
constraint set limits gross premium, short premium, stress loss, margin, collateral,
Greek concentration, and contract liquidity, then every economically nonzero direction
uses a finite budget. A direction that lies in the joint null space of every control must
carry no premium, payoff, or Greek exposure. It is economically empty. This separation
rules out free scale in the feasible option book.

For the joint state vector
\[
 Z_t=\begin{bmatrix}f_t\\ \varepsilon_t\end{bmatrix},
 \qquad
 \widehat S_Z=
 \begin{bmatrix}
 \widehat\Omega&\widehat\Gamma\\
 \widehat\Gamma^\top&\widehat\Sigma_\varepsilon
 \end{bmatrix},
\]
the option covariance estimator is
\[
 \widehat\Sigma_O=[B^G\ I]\widehat S_Z[B^G\ I]^\top.
\]
The map is positive semidefinite whenever the estimated joint covariance is positive
semidefinite. Expanding the product gives both factor and residual cross terms. With no
shrinkage, the construction reproduces the aligned sample option covariance before the
final numerical eigenvalue floor.

\subsection{Utility monotonicity and integer execution}

Define the cost-aware value
\[
 V(\mathcal C,c)=
 \max_{w\in\mathcal C}
 \left\{
 \widehat\mu^\top w
 -\frac{\gamma}{2}w^\top\Sigma w
 -c^\top|w|
 \right\}.
\]
If \(\mathcal C_1\subseteq\mathcal C_0\) and \(c_1\ge c_0\) componentwise, then
\(V(\mathcal C_1,c_1)\le V(\mathcal C_0,c_0)\). Every point feasible under the tighter
rules also belongs to the earlier feasible set, and its cost penalty is weakly larger.
The reporting direction is therefore conservative. Higher spreads or tighter execution
limits cannot improve the optimized value.

A continuous premium weight implies a contract count
\[
 N_i=\frac{w_iW_t}{m_iC_{i,t}}.
\]
The survival-first policies truncate counts toward cash and recheck the full constraint
set. They never remove one risky leg to manufacture feasibility. A failed direct
conversion selects cash and keeps the rejected book's diagnostics in the audit ledger.

\subsection{Maximum Sharpe and validation geometry}

For \(\Sigma\succ0\), the unconstrained maximum-Sharpe direction is proportional to
\(\Sigma^{-1}\mu\). Under a convex cone \(K\), its normalized form is
\[
 \max_{y\in K}\ y^\top\mu
 \quad\text{subject to}\quad
 y^\top\Sigma y\le 1.
\]
This reduction determines a direction but not an economically valid option scale. The
premium, stress, margin, and liquidity controls supply that scale. The earlier prototype
used the ratio as its selection target. The later policies use strictly concave net
utility and permit zero exposure.

Purged combinatorial cross-validation separates each test label from any training label
whose option holding window could overlap it. The fold ledger checks the realized calendar
gap on both sides of every test block. The selection rule excludes embargoed observations.
Recombined paths retain their original ordering within each test block and apply zero
wealth as an absorbing state.

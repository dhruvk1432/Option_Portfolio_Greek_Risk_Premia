\section{Licensed-quote execution audit}
\label{sec:execution_audit}

The execution audit uses a cost-capped licensed Databento acquisition covering February
2018--April 2026. It completed 1,170 requests and verified 82.0 million rows for \$17.68
against a \$40 cap. The pull contains 10-minute entry-close, next-open, and exit-close
windows, full-session VIX intervention days, and decision-date daily volume and open
interest.\footnote{The operational acquisition record, including the private-request-ledger
hash, is \path{analysis/artifacts/databento_audit/databento_acquisition_summary.json}.}

The quote source is consolidated minute BBO before March 28, 2023 and consolidated MBP-1
thereafter. The purpose is to audit the execution realism of the frozen R1 and R1.1
replays. No return, position, cost input, integerization rule, survival gate, or frozen
protocol is changed.

Positions from the frozen weight ledgers are matched to observed NBBO in each leg's
window. The audit then replaces only the spread evidence in three scenarios:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Mid} retains the frozen non-spread terms: fees, slippage, funding, and the
  recorded short-call borrow term.
  \item \emph{Touch} substitutes the observed entry and exit spreads into the frozen cost
  formula while leaving every other term unchanged.
  \item \emph{Worst} uses the entry-window maximum spread and the observed exit spread.
\end{itemize}
Months or legs without observed quotes retain the frozen modeled cost: they are counted,
never imputed. Rebuilding modeled cost from the frozen inputs reproduces
\verb|predicted_cost| exactly, with a maximum absolute gap below $10^{-16}$, so the
scenario comparison isolates the spread evidence.

R1.1 weight-aggregate entry coverage is 98.5 percent for larger, 96.2 percent for
larger+VIX, 98.7 percent for orig, and 94.1 percent for orig+VIX. Round-trip coverage is
62.9, 29.1, 65.1, and 25.5 percent, respectively. VIX options are cash settled at the
SOQ, so settlement-held positions have no exit-close quote. Where the cached holding path
contains a last-tradable-session quote, the audit uses it as a fallback and labels that
source in the ledger.

Mid fills are marginally better than the modeled series. At the touch, however, the
no-VIX orig book declines from 0.118 to 0.086 annualized return and from 1.097 to 0.830
Sortino. The orig+VIX book moves from 0.160 to 0.146 annualized return and from 2.275 to
2.052 Sortino; its worst scenario remains at 0.145 annualized return and 2.029 Sortino.
Observed spreads for the broad equity books are wider than the inferred-CBBO proxy
assumed in the frozen costs. Even so, the headline orig+VIX configuration survives the
harshest observed-quote scenario with Sortino approximately 2.0.

For R1.1 before March 28, 2023, the median observed relative spread is 2.4 percent versus
1.7 percent modeled, and the observed 90th percentile is 7.1 percent versus 2.7 percent
modeled. After the cutover, the corresponding observed/model medians are 4.4/1.5 percent
and the 90th percentiles are 12.4/2.4 percent. The split distinguishes minute-observable
consolidated BBO from the later consolidated MBP-1 record rather than treating the two
schemas as identical evidence.

Decision-mark accuracy is stable across the cutover. For R1.1, median absolute relative
error against observed entry mids is 2.9 percent before March 28, 2023 and 3.0 percent
after; the corresponding 90th percentiles are 13.8 and 13.0 percent.

R1.1's 90th-percentile participation is 2.8 percent of summed decision-date volume, 6.9
percent against the maximum publisher observation, and 0.5 percent of open interest. The
5 percent optimizer-volume, 10 percent capacity-volume, and 2 percent open-interest
diagnostics record 83/27/54 R1 breaches and 186/57/140 R1.1 breaches. These checks cover
entry dates only, not the holding path. R1 contract counts are implied at \$1 million NAV
through the R1.1 symbol lookup and are identified by the \verb|contracts_source| column;
R1.1 counts are the frozen integerized contracts.

The intervention artifact contains 156 evidence-only position rows, of which 154 have a
valid quote aggregate. Median quote-presence is 99.7 percent; across rows with quotes, the
median of the session median spreads is 2.7 percent and its cross-row 90th percentile is
12.9 percent. The cross-contract medians of first-valid and full-session VWAP touch are
5.250 and 4.531. These are post-freeze feasibility observations only. The risk-off arm
remains unscored under the frozen protocol.

The remaining limitations are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item pre-2023 option quotes are minute-observable, not tick-exact;
  \item no assignment/early-exercise validation;
  \item no corporate-action adjustments;
  \item volume/OI validated at entry dates only;
  \item quotes prove executability at displayed top-of-book size, not realized fills;
  \item the risk-off intervention arm remains unscored.
\end{itemize}

The audit strengthens cost realism of the retrospective development evidence; it does
not convert it into confirmatory evidence, and the 36-month prospective freeze stands.
